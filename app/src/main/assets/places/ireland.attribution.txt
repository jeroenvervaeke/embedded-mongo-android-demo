Modifications
-------------
Foursquare's data was transformed into the Overture schema by the Overture Maps
Foundation, who record that change as 2026-03-18. This extract makes two
further changes. It keeps only places whose primary category is one of the
categories named above, that fall inside the extent named above, that carry a
name and a geometry, and whose confidence is at least 0. It then
reshapes each record to _id, name, cat, brand, confidence, addr and a GeoJSON
loc point, dropping every other Overture column.
